[b][u]Enable Achievements[/u][/b]

Enables Sandustry's normal Steam achievement checks while playing with mods.

This mod does not unlock achievements directly. You must still meet each
achievement's normal in-game requirements.

[b][u]Compatibility[/u][/b]
This mod targets Sandustry 0.5.5. It relies on undocumented game state, so
future game updates may require a compatibility update.
