[b][u]Terraria's Last Prism reimagined as a Sandustry mining laser[/u][/b]

[b][u]IMPORTANT[/u][/b]
Subscribe, restart the game, and enable the mod before loading a save that contains the Last Prism.

This is intentionally an overpowered mod. It consumes no energy, and all upgrades are currently free.

[b][u]Controls[/u][/b]
[list]
[*]Hold left click to fire six rotating rainbow beams.
[*]The beams converge while charging.
[*]At full charge, they merge into one beam dealing their combined damage.
[*]Unlock Dark Spark to fire six revolving spread beams with right click.
[/list]

[b][u]Upgrades[/u][/b]
[list]
[*][b]Prismatic Damage[/b] - increases fully charged damage to 12, 18, and 24.
[*][b]Beam Thickness[/b] - increases visual beam width by 100% per level.
[*][b]Dark Spark[/b] - unlocks right-click spread fire.
[*][b]Ice Melting[/b] - allows the beams to melt ice into water.
[/list]

Check the Upgrades tab after receiving the item.

[b][u]Save warning[/u][/b]
Removing or disabling the mod while a save still contains the Last Prism may cause missing-item errors. Back up your save first.
